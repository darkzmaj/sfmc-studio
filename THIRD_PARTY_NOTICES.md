## Data

- **TF2 item metadata** (`public/catalog/*.json`) is generated by `scripts/build-catalog.mjs` from Valve's public TF2 item schema, fetched through the [tsuza/tf2-item-schema](https://github.com/tsuza/tf2-item-schema) mirror of the Steam Web API. English names may come from the builder's own TF2 installation (`resource/tf_english.txt`). Only factual metadata is stored: names, definition indexes, classes, equip regions, model paths, styles and paint colours. Item names, data and the Team Fortress 2 trademark belong to Valve Corporation.
